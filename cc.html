My Daily Routine Yesterday

Yesterday morning, I woke up at 7:30 a.m. After that, I freshened up and did some exercise. Then, I took a bath and had breakfast. After breakfast, I got ready and left for the office.

Then, I caught a bus and reached the office at 8:30 a.m. After arriving at the office, I had my bag checked and punched in for attendance. Then, I switched on my computer and practised typing. After that, I submitted a form and attended my mentor's class.

Next, I took an aptitude test and an assessment test. At 12:00 p.m., I had lunch. After lunch, I returned to the classroom and did my work. Then, my mentors taught us.

In the evening, I punched out at 6:00 p.m. After that, I caught a bus and returned to my room. When I arrived at my room, I took a bath and had dinner.

After dinner, I studied for two hours. Then, I used my phone for 30 minutes. Finally, I went to sleep.

My Last Holiday

Last holiday, I woke up at 9:00 a.m. After that, I cleaned my room and did some household chores. Then, I washed my clothes and took a bath.

After getting ready, I went out for a while to explore some places and enjoy myself. When I returned home, I studied for some time. After studying, I watched a movie and then worked on my laptop.

Later, I played some games to relax and enjoy my free time. In the evening, I went outside for a walk and spent some time outdoors.

At night, I returned to my room, had dinner, and relaxed for a while. Finally, I went to sleep.

Overall, I had a relaxing and productive holiday. I enjoyed my day and also made time for my studies.

How I Usually Spend My Holidays

On holidays, I usually wake up at around 9:00 a.m. After waking up, I clean my room and do some household chores. Then, I wash my clothes and take a bath.

After that, I sometimes go out to visit different places or spend some time outside. When I come back home, I study for a while to improve my knowledge.

After studying, I like to watch movies and work on my laptop. Sometimes, I also play games in my free time.

In the evening, I usually go outside for a walk or to spend some time with my friends. At night, I have dinner and prepare to go to bed.

I try to balance my studies, household responsibilities, and entertainment during my holidays. This helps me relax and make good use of my free time.

Holiday Questions & Answers

Q1. How did you spend your last holiday?

Answer: I spent my last holiday doing household chores, studying, watching a movie, and playing games. I also went outside in the evening. Overall, I had a relaxing and productive day.

Q2. What do you usually do on holidays?

Answer: I usually clean my room, wash my clothes, study, and work on my laptop. I also watch movies and play games in my free time. In the evening, I like to go outside for a walk.

Q3. Do you enjoy your holidays? Why?

Answer: Yes, I enjoy my holidays because I get time to relax, improve my knowledge, and do things that I enjoy. Holidays help me refresh my mind and prepare for the next working day.

One Important Grammar Tip

How do you spend your holidays? → Use the Simple Present Tense.

How did you spend your last holiday? → Use the Simple Past Tense.

Career preparation

Top 30 Common Job Interview Questions with Sample Answers

Note: These are sample answers. Replace blanks with your real details and adjust answers to match your actual skills and experience.

Section 1: Personal Introduction

01Tell me about yourself.

A: My name is ____. I am from ____. I have completed my ____. I am a hardworking and disciplined person. I have basic computer knowledge, and I am improving my English communication skills. I am looking for an opportunity to learn new things and build my career.

02What are your strengths?

A: My strengths are that I am hardworking, punctual, honest, and eager to learn new things. I always try to complete my work on time.

03What is your weakness?

A: Sometimes, I feel nervous while speaking English, but I am practising every day to improve my communication skills.

04Why should we hire you?

A: You should hire me because I am a quick learner, hardworking, and responsible. I am ready to learn new skills and give my best to the company.

05What are your hobbies?

A: My hobbies are learning new things, improving my computer skills, watching informative videos, and playing games in my free time.

Section 2: Education and Career

06Tell me about your educational background.

A: I have completed my ____ from ____. During my studies, I learned many useful things, and I am now focusing on improving my professional skills.

07Why do you want this job?

A: I want this job because it will give me an opportunity to learn, gain practical experience, and develop my career.

08Why do you want to join our company?

A: I want to join your company because I am looking for a professional environment where I can learn new skills, contribute to the team, and grow in my career.

09Are you a fresher or experienced?

A (if you are a fresher): I am a fresher, and I am excited to start my professional career. I am ready to learn from experienced people and work hard to improve my skills.

10Where do you see yourself in five years?

A: In five years, I see myself as a skilled and experienced professional with greater responsibilities. I want to keep learning and contribute to the growth of the company.

Section 3: Work and Behaviour

11Can you work under pressure?

A: Yes, I can work under pressure. I try to stay calm, manage my time properly, and complete my tasks according to their priority.

12Are you comfortable working in a team?

A: Yes, I am comfortable working in a team. I respect other people's opinions, communicate politely, and help my teammates whenever possible.

13What will you do if you make a mistake at work?

A: If I make a mistake, I will accept it, inform the concerned person if necessary, correct it, and learn from it to avoid repeating it.

14How do you manage your time?

A: I make a list of my tasks, set priorities, and try to finish my work within the given time.

15What motivates you?

A: Learning new things, improving my skills, achieving my goals, and making my family proud motivate me.

Section 4: Computer and Office-Related Questions

16Do you have computer knowledge?

A: Yes, I have basic computer knowledge. I can work with a computer, type documents, use the internet, and perform basic office tasks. I am also working on improving my computer skills.

17What is MS Excel?

A: MS Excel is a spreadsheet application used to organize data, perform calculations, create tables, and prepare reports.

18What is the difference between hardware and software?

A: Hardware refers to the physical parts of a computer, such as a keyboard, monitor, and mouse. Software refers to the programs and applications that run on a computer.

19What is data entry?

A: Data entry is the process of entering, updating, and maintaining information in a computer system accurately.

20How do you ensure accuracy in your work?

A: I carefully check the information I enter, review my work before submitting it, and correct any mistakes I find.

Section 5: Salary, Availability, and Final Questions

21What are your salary expectations?

A: As a fresher, my priority is to learn and gain experience. I am expecting a reasonable salary according to the company's standards and the responsibilities of the role.

22Can you work overtime if required?

A: Yes, I can work overtime when required, provided I have reasonable notice and the work arrangements are clear.

23Are you willing to relocate?

A (if true): Yes, I am willing to relocate for the right career opportunity.

Alternative: At present, I would prefer a position in my current location.

24Why should we select you over other candidates?

A: I believe I can be a good choice because I am dedicated, willing to learn, and serious about my responsibilities. I will work hard to meet the company's expectations.

25Do you have any questions for us?

A: Yes, thank you. I would like to know about the responsibilities of this role and the training opportunities available for new employees.

Section 6: Five More Common HR Questions

26What do you know about our company?

A: I understand that your company works in ____. I would like to learn more about its services and contribute to the team. (Research the actual company before your interview.)

27What does success mean to you?

A: Success means achieving my goals through hard work, continuous learning, and dedication.

28How do you handle feedback?

A: I take feedback positively because it helps me understand my mistakes and improve my performance.

29What would you do if you did not understand a task?

A: I would politely ask my senior or supervisor for clarification so that I could understand the task and complete it correctly.

30When can you join our company?

A (if available immediately): I can join immediately.

Alternative: I can join after completing my current commitments. (Choose the answer that is true for you.)

Mock Interview Practice

Interviewer: Good morning! Please introduce yourself and tell me a little about your education, skills, and career goals.

Practice tip: Answer in English using 4–6 sentences. Practise speaking naturally instead of memorizing every answer word for word. Give honest answers based on your real skills and experience.
